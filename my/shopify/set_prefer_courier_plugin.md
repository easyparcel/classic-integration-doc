# How to set prefer courier service in the plugin version
## Step 1: Open your Shopify store
login your Shopify store and find EasyParcel below the Apps.
<img width="1203" height="619" alt="Screenshot 2026-01-08 at 1 35 30 PM" src="https://github.com/easyparcel/nextgen-integration-doc/blob/509b2c104555ab1143d9866ab794b2b2c4470477/Pictures/WC-1.png" />
**note:** make sure that you have successfully integrate your Shopify store with EasyParcel.

## Step 2: Open Setting
Open **Setting -> Courier Setting ->** Click the **Add Zone** button.
<img width="1203" height="619" alt="Screenshot 2026-01-08 at 1 35 30 PM" src="https://github.com/easyparcel/nextgen-integration-doc/blob/509b2c104555ab1143d9866ab794b2b2c4470477/Pictures/WC-2.jpeg" />

## Step 3: Add Zone
Enter the zone name, and choose the destination
<img width="1203" height="619" alt="Screenshot 2026-01-08 at 1 35 30 PM" src="https://github.com/easyparcel/nextgen-integration-doc/blob/509b2c104555ab1143d9866ab794b2b2c4470477/Pictures/WC-3.jpeg" />
**Add-on:** The preferred courier list will be output in the checkout page based on the user’s address, and the available couriers will be shown **according to the destination**.

## Step 4: Save the Zone
save the zone detail by clicking the **Save** button
<img width="1203" height="619" alt="Screenshot 2026-01-08 at 1 35 30 PM" src="https://github.com/easyparcel/nextgen-integration-doc/blob/509b2c104555ab1143d9866ab794b2b2c4470477/Pictures/WC-4.jpeg" />

## Step 5: Add Courier Service
Once the zone is created, click Add Courier Service to start adding available couriers for that zone.
<img width="1203" height="619" alt="Screenshot 2026-01-08 at 1 35 30 PM" src="https://github.com/easyparcel/nextgen-integration-doc/blob/509b2c104555ab1143d9866ab794b2b2c4470477/Pictures/WC-5.jpeg" />

## Step 6: Add the prefer courier 
Set up each courier service with the following options:
**Courier Service** — Choose your preferred courier.
**Courier Display Name** — How it will appear to customers at checkout.
Shipping Rate Options:
1. **EasyParcel Member Rate** — Show your actual member rate.
2. **Add-on EasyParcel Member Rate** — Charge a slight additional fee or percentage.
3. **Fixed Rate** — Flat fee regardless of location.
<img width="1203" height="619" alt="Screenshot 2026-01-08 at 1 35 30 PM" src="https://github.com/easyparcel/nextgen-integration-doc/blob/509b2c104555ab1143d9866ab794b2b2c4470477/Pictures/WC-6.jpeg" />
**Add-on:** The courier that you have choose will be output in the checkout page.

## Step 7: Activate the Advanced Setting section
