# Combine orders feature

## Combine orders feature

- **Combine specific order** \- User can bulk select orders with same receiver address to combine to single shipment

- **Auto combine order** \- Users can also click the **Combine orders with same address **button to auto combine the orders that have the same receiver address

- **Undo Combine** \- After combined, the user can click the **Split** button to split the combined orders back to separate orders
